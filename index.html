Common Questions

FAQ

How do I get access to slice images? +
Two ways: subscribe monthly for unlimited slicing with no ads, or watch a single short rewarded ad to unlock full access for 24 hours. Either way gets you every feature in the app.
How long does the ad access last? +
Watching one rewarded ad gives you full access to all features for 24 hours from the time you watch it. Once it expires, simply watch another short ad or switch to a monthly subscription.
How do I cancel my subscription? +
Go to your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, and cancel Easy Image Slicer there. You keep access for the rest of the billing period you already paid for.
What grid sizes are supported? +
Preset grids like 2×2, 3×3, 4×4, 5×5 and more — plus a fully custom grid where rows and columns are individually editable.
Where are the sliced images saved? +
All sliced images are saved directly to your iPhone's Photos library. No cloud upload, no account needed.
Do I need an internet connection to slice? +
The slicing itself works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed to load rewarded ads (ad path) or to process subscription payments. Your photos are always processed on-device.
How can I report a bug or request a feature? +
Reach out via email at sreesudharsan.ss@gmail.com — the developer reads and responds to every message personally.
