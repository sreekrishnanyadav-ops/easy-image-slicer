Common Questions

FAQ

Is Easy Image Slicer really free? +
Yes, 100%. Every feature — all grid sizes, custom grids, export — is completely free with no hidden purchases or paywalls.
Are there any ads in the app? +
No. The app is completely ad-free during normal use. The only place ads appear is on the optional Support screen, where you can choose to watch one as a way to donate. It's entirely your choice.
What grid sizes are supported? +
You can use preset grids like 2×2, 3×3, 4×4, 5×5 and more. Rows and columns are individually editable, so you can create any custom grid combination that works for your needs.
Where are the sliced images saved? +
All sliced images are saved directly to your iPhone's Photos library. No cloud upload, no account needed.
Do I need an internet connection? +
Nope. The app works fully offline. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and never sent anywhere.
